The first computer bug was a real moth

The Story of The First Computer Bug

The first computer bug was a real moth. In 1947, Grace Hopper and her team found a moth stuck in the Harvard Mark II computer. It was causing errors.

They taped the moth into their logbook and wrote: "First actual case of bug being found." That is why today we say "debugging" when we fix code.

At Code Club Benin, we learn that bugs are normal. We find them, fix them, and keep building.

Quick Facts

  • Year: 1947
  • Computer: Harvard Mark II
  • Found by: Grace Hopper's team
  • Bug: A real moth taped to the logbook

Fun fact: The logbook is now in the Smithsonian Museum.
